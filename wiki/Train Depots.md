
The Train Depots serve as storage, maintenance, and training facilities for the server's railway network.

In addition to storing spare trains, the depots provide a location where new players can learn the basics of train operation before entering the main railway network.

## Overview

The Train Depots contain a collection of spare trains that are available for training and educational purposes.

New players are encouraged to visit the depots to familiarize themselves with train controls and railway operations.

---

## Learning To Drive Trains

The depots are intended to provide a safe environment for learning.

Players may use available trains to practice:

- Starting a train
    
- Stopping a train
    
- Basic train controls
    
- Station approaches
    
- General railway operations
    

---

## Spare Trains

Various trains may be stored at the depots when not in active service.

These trains may be used for training, testing, or future transportation projects.

---

## Purpose

The Train Depots exist to help new players become familiar with the railway system before operating trains elsewhere on the network.

The facilities also provide a convenient location for storing railway equipment.
