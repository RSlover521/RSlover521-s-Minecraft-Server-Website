
Factory District / River Valley Station serves the Factory District and River Valley regions and acts as one of the major transportation destinations on the server.

The station provides access to industrial facilities, manufacturing areas, and nearby community developments.

It is currently served by both the Mainline Train System and the Monorail System.

## Overview

Factory District / River Valley Station serves two closely connected areas:

- Factory District
- River Valley

The station functions as a transportation gateway for industrial activities and provides convenient access to one of the server's primary manufacturing regions.

---

## Transportation Services

### Mainline Train System

Provides long-distance transportation between major regions of the server.

Current route:

```text
Firberg
   │
Maple Fields
   │
River Valley
   │
Grassland
```

---

### Monorail System

Current route:

```text
Maple Forest
   │
Oak Village 1
   │
Maploom
   │
Factory District / River Valley
```

The station serves as the current eastern terminus of the Monorail System.

---

## Factory District

The Factory District contains various industrial facilities and production systems.

Many large-scale manufacturing projects are located within the district.

As the server continues to develop, additional factories and industrial infrastructure may be added.

---

## River Valley

River Valley is a growing regional settlement located near a large river system.

The area serves as an important destination within the transportation network and provides connections between industrial and residential developments.

---

## Monorail Production

The Factory District holds a special place in transportation history.

The region was responsible for the development and construction of the server's monorail infrastructure.

Many components and facilities associated with the Monorail System were developed within or near the Factory District.

As a result, the area is often associated with the growth and expansion of regional transportation projects.

---

## Significance

Factory District / River Valley Station serves as one of the primary industrial transportation hubs on the server.

The station connects manufacturing, transportation, and regional development into a single destination and remains an important part of the server's transportation network.
