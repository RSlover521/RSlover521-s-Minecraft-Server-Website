
This page contains known issues, limitations, and frequently reported problems on the RSlover521 Minecraft Server.

Not all issues have immediate solutions.

Some issues may be caused by Minecraft, mods, or third-party software.

---

## Performance

### Large Stations May Reduce FPS

Major transportation hubs, particularly Maple Fields Station, may reduce client FPS due to:

- Large structures
- Trains
- Display boards
- Create contraptions
- Block entities

Players experiencing performance issues may consider:

- Lowering render distance
- Lowering block entity distance
- Disabling shaders
- Using performance mods

---

## Modpack Compatibility

### Correct Modpack Version Required

Players must use the correct server modpack version.

Using outdated versions may result in:

- Connection failures
- Missing content
- Crashes

---

## Create Track Map

### Track Map May Occasionally Become Unavailable

The railway map depends on server-side services.

If the map becomes unavailable:

- Wait for services to restart
- Contact the server owner if the issue persists

---

## Seasonal Systems

### Snow Accumulation

Snow may accumulate during winter.

In some cases, snow may remain after winter has ended.

Snow cleanup operations may be required.

See:

- [[The Great Snow Crisis]]

---

## Transportation

### Missed Station Approaches

If a train driver fails to hold Space during a station approach, the train may continue past the station.

This is normal behavior.

Train drivers should pay attention to station approach notifications.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you discover a bug or issue not listed here, please contact RSlover521 through the Discord server.

Include:

- Description of the issue
- Screenshots if available
- Crash logs if applicable
- Steps to reproduce the problem

This helps identify and resolve issues more quickly.
