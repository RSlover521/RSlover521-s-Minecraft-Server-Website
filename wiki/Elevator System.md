
The Elevator System provides convenient vertical transportation between floors of buildings throughout the server.

Two types of vertical transportation systems are currently used:

- Escalators
- Elevators (Lifts)

---

## Escalators

Escalators provide automatic transportation between floors.

### How to Use

1. Walk onto the metal walkway.
2. Remain standing on the escalator.
3. Wait for the system to transport you to the destination floor.

No buttons or additional interaction are required.

## Elevators (Lifts)

Elevators provide access to multiple floors within a building.

### Calling the Elevator

If the elevator is not currently present:

1. Locate the elevator call button.
2. Press the button on the right side of the elevator entrance.
3. Wait for the elevator to arrive.

Once the elevator reaches your floor, the doors will automatically open.

## Entering the Elevator

1. Wait for the doors to open.
2. Walk inside the elevator cabin.
3. Locate the Contraption Control block.

## Selecting a Floor

The Contraption Control block is used to select your destination.

1. Look at the Contraption Control block.
2. Scroll the square selector to the desired floor.
3. Right-click to confirm your selection.

After a floor is selected, the elevator will begin moving automatically.

---

## Floor Information

Each floor displayed on the Contraption Control includes a description.

These descriptions help passengers quickly identify the correct destination.

Examples may include:

- Station Platforms
- Concourse Level
- Street Level
- Commercial Areas
- Observation Decks

## Tips

- Wait for the elevator to stop completely before exiting.
    
- Verify the floor description before making a selection.
    
- If the elevator is not present, use the call button before attempting to enter.
    
- Some buildings may contain different floor layouts and descriptions.
    

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### The elevator is not here.

Press the call button and wait for the elevator to arrive.

---

### The doors will not open.

Ensure the elevator has reached your floor completely before approaching the doors.

---

### How do I select a floor?

Use the Contraption Control block inside the elevator. Scroll to the desired floor and right-click to confirm your selection.

---

### How do I know which floor to choose?

Read the floor descriptions displayed on the Contraption Control block (Right below floor number). These descriptions identify the purpose of each floor.
