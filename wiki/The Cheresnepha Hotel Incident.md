
The Cheresnepha Hotel Incident was one of the earliest disciplinary incidents in the history of the RSlover521 Minecraft community.

The event occurred on the original vanilla server, prior to the creation of the current modded world.

The incident is notable for involving arson, a failed explanation, and one of the earliest known server bans.

---

## Background

During the era of the original vanilla server, players were actively constructing public buildings and community projects.

One of these projects was the Cheresnepha Hotel, a public building intended to serve the community.

At the time, the hotel was considered an important public structure.

---

## The Incident

At some point, a player intentionally set the Cheresnepha Hotel on fire.

The resulting fire caused damage to the public build and disrupted the surrounding area.

Because the hotel was a community project rather than a private structure, the incident was taken seriously.

---

## The Explanation

After the incident, the player responsible denied responsibility.

Instead, an alternative explanation was offered.

According to the player, a Pillager located in a boat near the hotel was responsible for setting the building on fire.

This explanation was met with skepticism.

Historical investigations have never produced evidence that the Pillager possessed either:

- Fire-starting capabilities
- Arson equipment
- A motive

As a result, the explanation was not accepted.

---

## Administrative Response

Following the incident, disciplinary action was taken.

The player was banned from the server.

This became one of the earliest recorded bans in server history.

---

## Historical Significance

The Cheresnepha Hotel Incident remains one of the most well-known stories from the original vanilla server era.

The event is frequently remembered for both the destruction of a public building and the unusual explanation that followed.

The incident also helped establish the server's long-standing policy regarding griefing and damage to public infrastructure.

---

## Legacy

The incident is remembered as one of the defining stories of the early server era.

It serves as a reminder that:

- Public builds should be respected.
- Griefing has consequences.
- Pillagers should not be used as legal defense witnesses.
