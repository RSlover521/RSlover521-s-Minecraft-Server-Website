## Can I build a base on this server?

Yes.

Find an available location and start building. Private builds are generally allowed, though land taxes may apply.

For more information, see [[Server Rules]].

---

## Can I build a museum?

Yes.

Public community projects are encouraged and are generally exempt from land taxes.

---

## Can I build a train station?

Yes.

Transportation infrastructure is welcome, especially if it benefits the community.

---

## Can I build a city or town?

Yes.

Whether you want to build a small village or an entire city, you are entirely free to do so.

---

## Can I create a secret project?

Yes.

The server owner enjoys surprises.

Please ensure your project follows the server rules and does not interfere with other players.

---

## Can I use Creative Mode?

Yes.

Players may build in either Survival Mode or Creative Mode.

---

## Can I use give commands?

Yes.

The server focuses on creativity, infrastructure, and community projects rather than strict survival progression.

---

## Can I destroy another player's build?

No.

This is one of the few questions with a very short answer.

Destroying another player's build or public infrastructure may result in an immediate ban.

See also: [[The Cheresnepha Hotel Incident]]
