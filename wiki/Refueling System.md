
The Refueling System allows players to replenish fuel in cars, aircraft, and other supported vehicles.

Most vehicles require fuel in order to operate.

---

## Before You Begin

Before refueling, ensure that:

- Your vehicle is parked near a fuel pump.
- You have sufficient funds available.
- You have already paid for fuel using the nearby Brass Depositor.

For fuel prices, see:

- [[Fuel Pricing]]

---

## Refueling Procedure

### Step 1

Park your vehicle near a fuel pump.

⚠️ Your vehicle must be within 16 blocks of the fuel pump.

![[Screenshot 2026-06-07 142441.png]]

---

### Step 2

Locate the Brass Depositor beside the fuel pump.

---

### Step 3

Pay the required fuel charge.

Fuel prices vary depending on the vehicle type.

For current pricing information, see:

- [[Fuel Pricing]]

---

### Step 4

After payment, right-click the fuel pump.

The vehicle will automatically begin receiving fuel.

---

### Step 5

Wait for fueling to complete.

Once fueling is finished, your vehicle is ready for use.

---

## Important Notes

⚠️ Vehicles located more than 16 blocks away from the fuel pump cannot be refueled.

⚠️ Payment must be completed before fueling.

⚠️ Different vehicle types may have different fuel prices.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My vehicle will not refuel.

Check that:

- The vehicle is within 16 blocks of the fuel pump.
- Payment has been completed.
- The vehicle supports refueling.

---

### Do I need to refill from empty?

No.

Vehicles may be refueled at any fuel level.

---

### Can I refuel multiple times?

Yes.

Players may refuel whenever necessary.
