
The Train Ticketing System is used to control access to train stations throughout the server.

Passengers are required to pay a fare before entering the station platform area.

---

## Before You Begin

You must have one of the following:

- Coins
- A valid Bank Card

For information on obtaining money and bank cards, see:

- [[Spurs]]
- [[Maple Bank]]

Without a valid payment method, you will not be able to enter the station.

---

### Step 2

Hold either:

- Coins
- A Bank Card

in your hand.

---

### Step 3

While holding the payment method, right-click the Brass Depositor.

The fare will be collected automatically.

---

### Step 4

After successful payment, the gate will temporarily unlock.

Quickly pass through the gate before it closes.

---

## Important Warning

⚠️ The gate remains unlocked for approximately **one second** after payment.

If you do not enter in time, the gate will close automatically.

You will need to pay again to reopen the gate.  
  
⚠️ You must hold the correct value of money as displayed on the Brass Depositor block. Otherwise you cannot pay.

However, if you have a bank card, it will automatically converts it to the correct value of spurs.

---

## Why Does The Gate Close So Quickly?

The short opening duration exists to prevent:

- Fare evasion
- Tailgating
- Multiple passengers entering using a single payment

Each passenger is expected to pay their own fare before entering the station.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I paid, but the gate closed before I entered.

You will need to pay again.

Try standing closer to the gate before making payment.

---

### Can multiple players enter after a single payment?

No.

The system is designed so that each passenger pays individually.

---

### Can I use either coins or a bank card?

Yes.

Both payment methods are accepted by the ticketing system.

---

### Do you have to pay again after exiting the train station?

No.

You have already paid before entering the station, simply walk out of the train station and enjoy the rest of your day!
