
The Metro System is the primary rapid-transit network of the RSlover521 Minecraft Server.

Metro lines connect major settlements, transportation hubs, and community landmarks throughout the world. Most metro routes operate underground, allowing for efficient travel without interfering with surface infrastructure.

Exceptions include Maple Fields Station and River Valley Station, which are constructed at ground level due to local terrain and city planning requirements.

## Metro 1-Line

The Metro 1-Line serves the eastern and western portions of the metro network and provides connections between several major settlements.

### Current Stations

1. Maple Town Metro Station
    - Formerly known as Oak Village 2 Metro Station
2. Oak Village 1 Metro Station
3. Maple Fields Station
4. River Valley Station
5. Spider House Metro Station

### Route Map

```text
Maple Town
    │
Oak Village 1
    │
Maple Fields
    │
River Valley
    │
Spider House
```

## Metro 2-Line

The Metro 2-Line connects northern and southern regions of the server and provides access to World Spawn.

### Current Stations

1. Firberg Metro Station
2. Crag Metro Station
3. Worldspawn Metro Station
4. Maple Forest Metro Station
5. Maple Fields Station

### Route Map

```text
Firberg
    │
Crag
    │
Worldspawn
    │
Maple Forest
    │
Maple Fields
```

## Transfer Stations

### Maple Fields Station

Maple Fields Station is currently the primary interchange station of the metro network.

Passengers may transfer between:

- Metro 1-Line
- Metro 2-Line
