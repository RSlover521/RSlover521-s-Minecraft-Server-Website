
Train operations on the RSlover521 Minecraft Server are governed by a set of railway rules, signs, and signaling systems.

All train drivers are expected to familiarize themselves with these regulations before operating trains on the railway network.

---

## General Rules

### Keep Right

All trains operate on the right-hand side of the railway whenever possible.

Train drivers should follow railway signs and routing instructions to ensure they remain on the correct track.

Failure to do so may result in entering the wrong railway line or disrupting railway operations.

---

Dead End Warning Signs warn train drivers that a railway terminates ahead.

## Railway Signals

Railway signals are used to indicate track occupancy and route availability.

Three signal types are currently used.

---

### Double Semaphore Signals

Commonly found on:

- Mainline Railways
- Railway Splits
- Major Junctions

Double Semaphores have three aspects:

#### Green

Track ahead is clear.

Proceed normally.

![[Screenshot 2026-06-07 071121.png]]

#### Yellow

An intersection exists ahead and at least one route is occupied.

Proceed with caution.

![[Pasted image 20260607071423.png]]

#### Red

The next rail segment is occupied.

Do not proceed.

### Turn Signal Lights

Turn Signal Lights are special railway signals used at track junctions and turns.

These signals indicate whether it is safe to enter a particular route while turning.

Turn Signal Lights consist of:

- A railway signal (Semaphore or Nixie Tube)
- Directional arrows indicating the route being monitored

#### Purpose

Turn Signal Lights help train drivers determine whether another train is occupying the route they intend to enter.

This is especially useful at:

- Railway junctions
- Complex intersections
- Track merges
- Route splits

#### Signal Meanings

##### Green

The selected route is clear.

The train may proceed into the indicated route.

![[Pasted image 20260607142213.png]]
##### Red

The selected route is occupied.

Do not proceed.

Train drivers should wait until the signal clears before entering the route.

![[Pasted image 20260607142145.png]]

#### Example

In this example, the arrows indicate the route being monitored.

Drivers should observe both the signal aspect and the directional arrows before making a turn.

## Station Approaches

When approaching a station, train drivers may see the message:

```text
Hold Space to Approach [Station Name]
```

### Stopping at a Station

Hold the Space key when the message appears.

This allows the train to enter station approach mode and stop at the platform.

### Passing Through a Station

If the train is not scheduled to stop, no action is required.

The train may continue normally.

---

## Driver Responsibility

Train drivers are expected to:

- Follow railway signs
- Observe railway signals
- Know their route
- Use station approaches correctly
- Operate trains safely

Failure to do so may result in confusion, missed turns, or accidental visits to locations you never intended to reach.

## Questions and Assistance

The railway network can be complex, especially for new train drivers.

If you have questions regarding:

- Railway signs
- Railway signals
- Route planning
- Station approaches
- Train operations

please contact the server owner through the Discord server.

Additional assistance may also be available from experienced train operators and community members.

Remember:

When in doubt, stop the train and ask.

It is significantly easier than accidentally arriving at the wrong end of the railway network.
