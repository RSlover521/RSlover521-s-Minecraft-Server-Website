
Vehicles on the RSlover521 Minecraft Server require fuel in order to operate.

Fuel can be purchased at designated fuel stations throughout the server.

---

## Fuel Prices

### 🚗 Car Fuel

Price:

```text
4 Bevels
```

---

### ✈️ Aircraft Fuel

Price:

```text
6 Bevels
```

---

## How Fueling Works

Fuel pricing is based on a fixed rate.

The same amount is charged regardless of the vehicle's current fuel level.

### Examples

The following situations cost the same amount:

- Empty → Full
- Half Full → Full
- Nearly Full → Full

Players are encouraged to refuel before long trips to avoid running out of fuel.

---

## Refueling Procedure

### Step 1

Park your vehicle near the fuel pump.

⚠️ The vehicle must be within 16 blocks of the fuel pump.

---

### Step 2

Locate the Brass Depositor beside the fuel pump.

![[Pasted image 20260607142445.png]]

---

### Step 3

Pay the required amount using the Brass Depositor.

---

### Step 4

After payment, right-click the fuel pump to begin fueling.

The vehicle will automatically receive fuel.

---

## Important Notes

⚠️ Vehicles located more than 16 blocks away from the fuel pump cannot be refueled.

⚠️ Fuel prices are fixed and do not depend on the amount of fuel added.

---

## Fueling Without Payment

Players are expected to pay for all fuel received.

Failure to pay after fueling is considered fare evasion.

### Penalty

Players who receive fuel without paying will be charged a fine of:

```text
4 Sprockets
(8 Bevels)
```

Server staff may issue additional penalties for repeated offenses.
