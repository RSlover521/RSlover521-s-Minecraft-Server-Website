
Maple Forest Metro Station is a station located within the Maple Forest region and serves passengers traveling on the Metro 2-Line.

The station provides convenient access to Maple Forest and nearby developments.

## Overview

Maple Forest Metro Station is one of the stations on the Metro 2-Line and serves as a local transportation stop for the surrounding area.

While it is smaller than some of the server's major transportation hubs, it remains an important connection within the transportation network.

## Historical Significance

Although Maple Forest Metro Station primarily functions as a local metro station, the surrounding area holds an important place in transportation history.

The Maple Forest region was the location of the first monorail station ever constructed on the server.

This marked the beginning of the Monorail System and the eventual expansion of regional monorail transportation across the server.
