
Worldspawn Metro Station serves the World Spawn region and provides new and existing players with access to the server's transportation network.

The station is located near the world's spawn point and is often one of the first transportation facilities encountered by new players.

---

## Overview

Worldspawn Metro Station is a station on the Metro 2-Line and serves as the primary transportation gateway for new arrivals.

Because of its location near World Spawn, the station provides convenient access to other regions of the server without requiring long-distance travel on foot.

---

## Rail Services

### Metro 2-Line

Current route:

```text
Firberg
   │
Crag
   │
Worldspawn
   │
Maple Forest
   │
Maple Fields
```

Passengers may use the Metro 2-Line to travel between Worldspawn and other connected destinations.

---

## Free Transportation

Worldspawn Metro Station is unique within the transportation network.

### No Ticket Required

Passengers may enter and use the station free of charge.

Benefits include:

- No train ticket required
- No Spurs required
- No hidden fees
- Immediate access for new players

This policy exists to help new players travel throughout the server and begin exploring the world.

---

## New Player Access

Worldspawn Metro Station is intended to provide new arrivals with an easy introduction to the server's transportation system.

Players who have recently joined the server can use the station to travel to:

- Maple Forest
- Maple Fields
- Firberg
- Crag
- Other connected destinations

without needing money or transportation experience.

---

## Significance

Worldspawn Metro Station serves as the gateway between World Spawn and the rest of the transportation network.

Many players begin their first railway journey from this station.
