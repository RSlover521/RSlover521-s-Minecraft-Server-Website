
_What is a spur?_

Spurs are the primary currency used throughout the RSlover521 Minecraft Server economy.

They are used to purchase transportation services, trade with vendors, and conduct business with other players.

Many systems throughout the server rely on Spurs and related currencies for payments and transactions.

---

## Currency Units

The server economy uses multiple denominations of currency.

### Currency Conversion

|Currency|Value|
|---|---|
|Spur|1 Spur|
|Bevel|8 Spurs|
|Sprocket|16 Spurs|
|Cog|64 Spurs|
|Crown|8 Cogs|
|Sun|64 Cogs|

Higher denominations are intended to simplify larger transactions and reduce the number of individual coins required.

---

## Using Physical Currency

Many machines on the server use physical currency items.

Examples include:

- Brass Depositors
- Vendors

When paying with physical currency, the exact denomination required must be used.

### Example

If a Brass Depositor requires:

```
1 Spur
```

then the player must hold Spurs.

The machine will not automatically exchange:

- Bevels
- Sprockets
- Cogs
- Crowns
- Suns

into the correct amount.

For example, a Brass Depositor requesting 1 Spur will not accept a Bevel, even though a Bevel is worth more than 1 Spur.

This limitation exists because most machines are unable to perform currency conversion automatically.

## Bank Cards

Bank Cards provide a convenient alternative to carrying physical currency.

Instead of holding coins, players may store money in a bank account and use a Bank Card for purchases.

When a Bank Card is used:

1. The required amount is automatically calculated.
2. The correct value is withdrawn from the player's account.
3. Payment is completed automatically.

As long as sufficient funds are available, the player does not need to worry about denominations or currency conversion.

## Numismatics Wallets

Numismatics Wallets provide a convenient way to store and manage physical currency.

Instead of carrying multiple stacks of coins, players may deposit their currency into a wallet.

A wallet allows players to:

- Store large amounts of currency
- Reduce inventory usage
- Withdraw specific amounts of money when needed
- Organize physical currency more efficiently

Unlike Brass Depositors and Vendors, wallets are capable of handling currency conversion internally.

For example, a player may store Bevels, Sprockets, Cogs, Crowns, and Suns inside a wallet and later withdraw the exact number of Spurs required for a transaction.

Wallets are often used alongside Bank Cards as a convenient way to manage money while traveling.

For more information, see:

## Why Use A Bank Card?

Bank Cards offer several advantages:

- Automatic currency conversion
- Convenient payments
- Reduced inventory usage
- Faster transactions
- No need to carry multiple denominations

Many experienced players use Bank Cards as their primary payment method.
