
The Mainline Train System provides long-distance transportation between major regions of the RSlover521 Minecraft Server.

Unlike the Metro System, which primarily serves local and urban travel, the Mainline Train System is designed for inter-regional transportation and connects distant settlements across the world.

Mainline railways operate primarily above ground and allow passengers to travel efficiently between major communities and development areas.

## Overview

The Mainline Train System serves as one of the primary transportation networks on the server.

The network is intended for longer-distance travel and connects major settlements that are separated by significant distances.

Passengers may use the Mainline Train System to travel between population centers, industrial regions, and developing communities.

## Service Benefits

The Mainline Train System offers:

- Long-distance transportation between regions
- Efficient travel across the server
- Connections between major settlements
- Integration with other transportation systems
- Scenic above-ground journeys

---

## Connections

Passengers may transfer to other transportation systems at selected stations.

Available connections may include:

- Metro System
- Monorail System
- Local transportation services
