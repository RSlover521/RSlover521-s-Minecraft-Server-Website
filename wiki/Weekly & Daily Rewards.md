
Weekly and Daily Rewards provide players with a steady source of income and are available through the Quest Book.

Players are encouraged to claim these rewards regularly to help build their finances and participate in the server economy.

---

## Accessing the Quest Book

There are two ways to open the Quest Book:

### Method 1

Craft a Quest Book and use it directly.

---

### Method 2

Open your inventory and click the Quest Book button located in the upper-left corner of the screen.

## Claiming Rewards

### Step 1

Open the Quest Book.

---

### Step 2

On the left side panel, navigate to the:

```text
Weekly & Daily Claims
```

chapter.

![[Pasted image 20260607142602.png]]

---

### Step 3

Locate either:

- Daily Claim
- Weekly Claim

---

### Step 4

Click the white checkmark to claim the reward.

Once claimed, the reward will be delivered automatically.

## Reward Resets

Daily and Weekly Rewards reset periodically.

When a reset occurs, players may claim the reward again.

Server announcements will notify players when rewards have been refreshed.

---

## How To Know When Rewards Reset

Reward resets are announced through the server chat system.

Players should keep an eye on in-game announcements for information regarding:

- Daily Claim Resets
- Weekly Claim Resets
- Special Reward Events

---

## Tips

💰 Claim rewards regularly to build your account balance.

💰 New players are strongly encouraged to claim rewards as one of their first sources of income.

💰 Weekly rewards generally provide larger payouts than daily rewards.
