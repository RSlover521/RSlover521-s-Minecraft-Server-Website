## Step 1: Explore Spawn

World Spawn contains several important facilities:

- Transportation stations
- Public buildings
- Information signs
- Community infrastructure

Feel free to explore the area before beginning your adventure.

## Step 2: Find a Place to Live

One of the core principles of this server is freedom.

Players are free to establish a base almost anywhere in the world, provided the location is not already occupied or reserved.

You may:

- Build a small starter house
- Establish a large industrial complex
- Create a town or city
- Build transportation infrastructure
- Contribute public projects

For more information, see [[Server Rules]].

## Step 3: Earn Your First Spurs

Spurs are the primary currency used throughout the server.

You can earn Spurs through:

- Mining and selling resources
- Participating in the economy
- Trading with other players
- Community activities

For more information, see [[Spurs]] and [[Maple Bank]].

## Step 4: Learn the Transportation Network

The server features an expanding transportation system, including:

- Metro lines
- Monorail lines
- Railways
- Elevators

Many destinations can be reached without building your own transportation infrastructure.

See:

- [[Metro System]]
- [[Monorail System]]
- [[Mainline Train System]]
- [[Train Ticketing System]]

## Step 5: Start Building

Once you have found a suitable location, begin building!

Players may build in either:

- Survival Mode
- Creative Mode

The server focuses on creativity, engineering, infrastructure, and community projects.

There is no required progression path. Play the game however you enjoy it.
