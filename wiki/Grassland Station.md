
Grassland Station is a station on the Mainline Train System and serves the Grassland region.

Located within a vast plains environment, the station provides access to open landscapes, natural scenery, and one of the quieter regions of the server.

## Overview

Grassland Station serves as a destination along the Mainline Train System and provides transportation access to the surrounding plains.

Unlike some stations that were developed around large settlements, industrial districts, or transportation hubs, Grassland Station primarily serves the natural environment and nearby developments.

---

## Rail Services

### Mainline Train System

Current route:

```text
Firberg
   │
Maple Fields
   │
River Valley
   │
Grassland
```

Grassland Station currently serves as the eastern terminus of the Mainline Train System.

## Scenic Features

### Open Plains

The Grassland region is known for its wide open spaces and unobstructed views.

Visitors may enjoy:

- Expansive grasslands
- Open horizons
- Natural landscapes
- Scenic railway journeys

---

### Camping Opportunities

The surrounding area provides an excellent environment for camping and outdoor activities.

Players looking for a peaceful destination away from major population centers may find Grassland Station to be an ideal location.

---

## Atmosphere

Grassland Station offers a quieter experience compared to larger transportation hubs such as Maple Fields.

The station is often associated with relaxation, exploration, and enjoying the natural beauty of the surrounding region.

---

## Significance

Grassland Station demonstrates that not every station must be built around industry, transportation, or large-scale development.

Sometimes a station exists simply because a destination is worth visiting.

---

### Historical Significance

Grassland Station is one of the older stations on the server and was part of the original Mainline Train System.

During the early days of the railway network, the Mainline route operated as:

```text
Firberg
   │
Maple Fields
   │
Grassland
```

before later expansions introduced additional stations and destinations.

Unlike many other regions that experienced significant redevelopment over time, the area surrounding Grassland Station has remained largely unchanged since its construction.

As a result, the station serves as a rare snapshot of an earlier era of the server's transportation history.
