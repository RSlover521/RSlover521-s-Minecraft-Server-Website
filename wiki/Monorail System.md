
The Monorail System is a rapid-transit service designed to provide efficient connections between major settlements while offering panoramic views of the surrounding landscape.

Unlike the Metro System, which primarily operates underground, the Monorail System travels above ground and allows passengers to observe the server's cities, forests, and industrial districts during their journey.

## Overview

The Monorail Line connects several important destinations across the region and serves as a convenient alternative to the Metro System for longer-distance travel.

The line was officially opened as part of the server's ongoing transportation expansion program.

One of its primary advantages is the direct connection between Maple Forest and Oak Village, eliminating the need to transfer through Maple Fields Station.

---

## Current Stations

### 🚝 Maple Forest Monorail Station

Located near Maple Forest and surrounding residential areas.

---

### 🚝 Oak Village 1 Monorail Station

Provides access to Oak Village and serves as a major transfer point between transportation systems.

---

### 🚝 Maploom Monorail Station

Serves the Maploom region, named after the nearby maple and cherry forest areas.

---

### 🚝 Factory District / River Valley Monorail Station

Provides access to the Factory District and River Valley developments.

## Service Benefits

The Monorail System offers:

- Direct travel between Maple Forest and Oak Village
- Scenic above-ground views
- Fast connections between major settlements
- Access to industrial and residential districts
- Integration with the server's broader transportation network
