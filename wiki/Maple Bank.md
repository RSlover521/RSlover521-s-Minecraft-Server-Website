
Maple Bank is the primary financial institution of the RSlover521 Minecraft Server.

The bank provides mineral exchange services, banking services, business services, and account management for players throughout the server.

Many economic systems on the server rely on Maple Bank and its services.

---

## Location

Maple Bank is located at:

```text
X: -164
Y: 66
Z: -244
```

Players may conveniently access Maple Bank by:

- Metro 1-Line → Oak Village 1 Station
- Metro 1-Line → Maple Town Station
- Monorail → Oak Village 1 Monorail Station

## Mineral Exchange Service

Located on the left side of the bank lobby.

Players may exchange minerals and resources for Spurs and other currency denominations.

## Banking Services

Located toward the rear of the bank.

Three Bank Terminals are available for public use.

These terminals may be used to:

- Deposit money into a bank account
- Withdraw money from a bank account
- Manage personal funds

For more information, see:

- [[Bank Cards]]
- [[Spurs]]

---

## Service Provider Window

Located on the right side of the bank.

Certain services require assistance from a human employee.

To access these services, contact:

- The Server Owner
- A Server Administrator

### Available Services

- New Bank Card Applications
- Replacement Bank Cards
- New ID Card Applications
- Business Registration
- Shared Business Accounts
- Business Account Access
- Other Administrative Services

Availability may depend on whether a qualified employee is currently online.

---

## Business Banking

Businesses may maintain shared accounts that can be accessed by multiple authorized members.

Business banking services are available through the Service Provider Window.

For more information, contact a server administrator.

---

## Notes

- Maple Bank is the primary financial institution on the server.
- Mineral exchange rates may change over time.
- Some services require human approval and cannot be completed automatically.
- Business services may require multiple participants.
