
Crag Metro Station serves the Crag region and is one of the stations located on the Metro 2-Line.

The station provides access to one of the most scenic natural landscapes on the server, featuring dramatic cliffs, deep valleys, and large cave systems.

## Overview

Crag Metro Station serves as a local stop on the Metro 2-Line and provides convenient transportation access to the surrounding region.

The area is known for its rugged terrain and impressive natural scenery, making it a popular destination for sightseeing and exploration.

## Scenic Attractions

### Large Cave Systems

The Crag region contains several large cave formations and underground features.

Visitors may explore extensive cave networks and observe natural geological formations throughout the area.

---

### Cliffs and Mountain Terrain

One of the defining characteristics of the region is its dramatic landscape.

The area features:

- Large cliffs
- Deep valleys
- Elevated terrain
- Scenic overlooks

These natural features provide excellent views of the surrounding environment.

---

### Sightseeing

Crag Metro Station is often used as a convenient access point for players interested in exploration, photography, and sightseeing.

The surrounding landscape offers some of the most impressive natural views accessible by rail.

## Significance

While Crag Metro Station is not one of the largest stations on the server, it serves as an important gateway to one of the server's most distinctive natural landscapes.

The station is particularly notable for its access to scenic terrain and geological features.
