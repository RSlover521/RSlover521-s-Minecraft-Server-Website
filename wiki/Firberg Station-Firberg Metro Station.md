
Firberg is a transportation district located within the greater Crag region and serves as one of the northern gateways to the server's railway network.

The area is known for its dramatic terrain, featuring high cliffs, deep ravines, and rugged landscapes similar to those found throughout the Crag region.

Firberg is unique in that it contains both a Mainline Train station and a Metro station, allowing passengers to transfer between transportation systems.

## Overview

Firberg serves as an important transportation node within the northern portion of the server.

Unlike most locations, Firberg is served by two separate railway stations:

- Firberg Station (Mainline Train System)
- Firberg Metro Station (Metro 2-Line)

The two stations are connected, allowing passengers to transfer between regional and local rail services.

---

## Transportation Services

### Firberg Station

Part of the Mainline Train System.

Provides long-distance rail service to other regions of the server.

## Station Layout

Firberg is one of the few locations where passengers may transfer directly between Mainline and Metro services.

This makes Firberg an important interchange point for travelers moving between transportation systems.

---

## Dual Exit Design

Firberg Metro Station features a unique two-exit layout.

Passengers may leave the station through either exit depending on their destination.

This design improves access to surrounding areas and provides greater flexibility for travelers.

---

## Scenic Features

### Cliffs and Ravines

Firberg shares many geographical characteristics with the neighboring Crag region.

The area is known for:

- Tall cliffs
- Deep ravines
- Elevated terrain
- Scenic viewpoints

Visitors may enjoy dramatic landscapes while traveling through the region.

---

## Significance

Firberg serves as both a transportation hub and a gateway to the northern regions of the server.

Its combination of Mainline and Metro services makes it one of the more strategically important stations within the railway network.

The station's unique transfer capabilities and dual-exit design distinguish it from many other stations on the server.
