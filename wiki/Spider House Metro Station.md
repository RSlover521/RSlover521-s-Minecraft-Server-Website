
Spider House Metro Station serves the Spider House region and acts as the northern terminus of the Metro 1-Line.

Unlike many stations that primarily serve cities or transportation hubs, Spider House Metro Station was constructed specifically to provide access to a player's base and nearby facilities.

![[Pasted image 20260607145749.png]]

---

## Overview

Spider House Metro Station is located near Spider House, a player-developed settlement and operations center.

The station provides convenient access to several important facilities and allows passengers to travel directly between Spider House and the rest of the Metro 1-Line network.

---

---

## Nearby Facilities

### Villager Trade Center (VTC)

The Villager Trade Center provides access to villager trading services and resources.

Players may visit the facility to conduct trades and obtain useful items.

---

### Great Monster Farm (GMF)

The Great Monster Farm is a large-scale mob farming facility located near Spider House.

The facility provides resources obtained from hostile mobs and serves as one of the major utility structures in the area.

---

## Purpose

Unlike some stations that were built to support city growth or transportation expansion, Spider House Metro Station was constructed specifically to improve access to an established player base and its supporting infrastructure.

The station demonstrates how the transportation network continues to expand in response to community development.

---

## Significance

Spider House Metro Station highlights one of the key goals of the transportation network:

Connecting players to the places that matter.

By providing direct rail access to important player facilities, the station helps integrate community projects into the wider server transportation system.
