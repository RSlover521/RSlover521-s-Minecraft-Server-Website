
Vehicles provide an alternative method of transportation throughout the server.

Players may use cars, trucks, aircraft, and other supported vehicles to travel between destinations.

Unless otherwise specified, vehicle operation generally follows United States traffic conventions.

---

## Driving Side

All vehicles should travel on the **right side** of the road whenever possible.

When multiple lanes are available:

- Keep right under normal conditions.
- Use other lanes only when necessary.
- Return to the right lane after passing.

---

## Speed and Safety

Players are expected to operate vehicles responsibly.

Drivers should:

- Avoid collisions with other vehicles.
- Slow down in populated areas.
- Yield when appropriate.
- Follow road markings and signs when present.

---

## Fuel Requirements

Most vehicles require fuel to operate.

For information regarding fuel prices and refueling procedures, see:

- [[Fuel Pricing]]

---

## Parking

When leaving a vehicle unattended:

- Park in designated parking areas when available.
- Avoid blocking roads, intersections, and station entrances.
- Avoid obstructing other players.

---

## Aircraft

Aircraft should only take off and land in areas with sufficient space.

Pilots are encouraged to use airports, airfields, and other suitable facilities whenever available.

---

## Enforcement

Most traffic rules are intended to promote safety and improve the experience of all players.

Minor mistakes are generally tolerated.

However, intentionally disrupting traffic, blocking infrastructure, or repeatedly causing problems may result in administrative action.

---

## Common Sense Rule

If an action would be considered unsafe, disruptive, or unreasonable in real life, it is probably not a good idea on the server either.

When in doubt, use common sense and be considerate of other players.
