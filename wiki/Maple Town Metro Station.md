
Maple Town Metro Station serves Maple Town and the surrounding region and is one of the stations located on the Metro 1-Line.

The station provides convenient access to several important destinations, including Maple Town, Maple Bank, and Oak Village 1.

## Overview

Maple Town Metro Station serves as one of the western stations on the Metro 1-Line and acts as a gateway to several important locations within the server.

The station is frequently used by players traveling to Maple Town and nearby services.

---

## Rail Services

### Metro 1-Line

Current route:

```text
Maple Town
   │
Oak Village 1
   │
Maple Fields
   │
River Valley
   │
Spider House
```

Passengers may use the Metro 1-Line to travel between Maple Town and other destinations throughout the transportation network.

---

## Nearby Destinations

### Maple Town

The station provides direct access to Maple Town and its surrounding developments.

---

### Maple Bank

Maple Bank is located within walking distance of the station.

Players visiting the bank for financial services, mineral exchanges, or account management may use Maple Town Metro Station for convenient access.

---

### Oak Village 1

Oak Village 1 is located nearby and can be reached easily from the station.

This provides access to one of the oldest and most important settlements on the server.

---

## Station History

Maple Town Metro Station was originally known as:

```text
Oak Village 2 Metro Station
```

During the development of Maple Town, the surrounding area was significantly redeveloped.

The original village was removed to create space for the growing town, and the station was subsequently renamed to better reflect the area's new identity.

The station remains one of the few locations on the server to have undergone an official name change.

---

## Significance

Maple Town Metro Station serves as an important transportation gateway for several major destinations.

Its proximity to Maple Town, Maple Bank, and Oak Village 1 makes it one of the more strategically useful stations within the Metro network.
