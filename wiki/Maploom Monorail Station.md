
Maploom Monorail Station is a station on the Monorail System that serves the forests and surrounding regions between Oak Village and the Factory District.

The station provides convenient access to nearby natural areas and serves as an intermediate stop on the Monorail Line.

## Overview

Maploom Monorail Station is located within a region known for its forests and natural scenery.

The station was constructed as part of the Monorail System expansion and serves as a connection point between western and eastern destinations along the route.

---

## Monorail Service

Current route:

```text
Maple Forest
   │
Oak Village 1
   │
Maploom
   │
Factory District / River Valley
```

Passengers may use the Monorail System to travel between Maploom and other connected destinations.

---

## Name Origin

The station's name is derived from nearby natural features.

The surrounding area contains both:

- Maple Forests
- Cherry Forests

The name **Maploom** combines elements of these landscapes into a single station name.

The station serves as a gateway to one of the more scenic portions of the Monorail Line.

---

## Scenic Features

The area surrounding Maploom is known for:

- Maple forests
- Cherry forests
- Natural landscapes
- Seasonal scenery

Passengers traveling through the station may enjoy views of the surrounding wilderness.

---

## Significance

Maploom Monorail Station serves as an important intermediate stop on the Monorail System.

While it is smaller than major transportation hubs such as Maple Fields, it plays a valuable role in connecting communities along the Monorail Line.

The station is also notable for its distinctive name and scenic surroundings.
